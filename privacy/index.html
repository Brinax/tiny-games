Privacy Policy

Tiny Games: Prism Ripple and Wake Cutter. Last updated: October 14, 2026.

Android app

The Prism Ripple Android app collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and makes no network requests. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Web version

The browser versions of the games on this site use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visit counter. It sets no cookies, does not track you across sites and stores no personal information. It only counts page views with basic details such as the page, referrer, browser type and screen size. Your IP address is not stored.

Game progress is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

Children

The games do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
